# Known Limitations

## Scientific

1. Common facility-level measured moisture and LHV data were unavailable for all three countries.
2. WFQRI weights, anchors, thresholds, and several missing-data substitutions were researcher-defined and not calibrated against a common measured feedstock-quality outcome.
3. Country waste composition and single-city weather proxies operate at different geographic scales.
4. Singapore uses disposed-waste composition and aggregate WtE information rather than facility-level intake moisture, LHV, electricity, and efficiency.
5. The historical Indonesia 2025 aggregation used inconsistent composition weighting, and one municipality contained a physically impossible reported component sum. Later audit corrections are not silently substituted into this historical snapshot.
6. The stored final Seoul regression can be reconstructed from its merged panel, but the original end-to-end generator for the exact final Table 15 version was not found.
7. Regression associations do not establish that rainfall causes waste moisture, LHV, or facility-performance changes.

## Reproducibility

1. The compact repository reproduces the historical endpoint, not every upstream download and raw-data cleaning step.
2. Network collection scripts and literature-PDF extraction are excluded because of API drift, local-path dependencies, and redistribution concerns.
3. Twenty-six automated outputs reproduce exactly against the frozen release references. In the wider archive, two READMEs were later amended by hand and two additional reports were not generated by the scripts.
4. Exact historical Node and PowerShell versions were not captured in May 2026. Release preparation succeeded with Node.js 24.19.0 and PowerShell 7.6.4.
